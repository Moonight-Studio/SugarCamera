Sugar Camera · Support

Support

Take a photo or import one with the system photo picker, choose the original or one of six light filters, and save the result to your photo library.

Using filters

Capture or import a photo first, then choose Original, Natural, Clear, Sweet, Cool White, Film, or Warm Sun. Filters are applied after capture or import; the live camera preview does not show them yet.

Permissions

Allow camera access to take photos and add-only photo library access to save results. If an action fails, check the app permissions in iOS Settings. The system photo picker provides only the image you select.

Contact support

Open a GitHub issue with your device model, iOS version, steps to reproduce, and any error message. Issues are public; do not post private photos, contact information, or other sensitive content.

You can use the same issue tracker for privacy questions. Read our privacy policy.
